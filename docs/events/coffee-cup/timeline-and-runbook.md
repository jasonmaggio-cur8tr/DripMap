# Timeline, To-Do List & Run of Show

**Target game day: Sunday, Nov 15, 2026 — or a spring 2027 edition. The date is decided on Friday, Oct 9.**

## The Oct 9 decision gate

Nov 15 is seven weeks out, which is enough only if the venue locks in week one. Rather than guess now, work the Nov 15 plan for two weeks and decide on **Friday, Oct 9** with real information instead of a feeling:

| On Oct 9 | Decision |
|---|---|
| **8+ shops committed and Futsi confirmed** | Order the trophy. Run Nov 15 lean and self-funded. |
| **Under 8 shops, or Futsi's Sunday is booked** | Slide to early March 2027 with warm shops already in hand, a sponsor deck written, and nothing spent. |

**Everything you do before Oct 9 transfers to either date** — pitching shops, the Futsi conversation, the sponsor deck, the shirt design, registration on DripMap. The first irreversible spend is the trophy, so there is no cost to trying for November and real value in the information.

If it slides, pick the spring date deliberately: avoid Super Bowl Sunday, the March Madness opening weekend and spring break. An early-to-mid March Sunday sets up an annual November rhythm from year two on.

## Week 0 — Sept 25 → Oct 2 · LOCK THE VENUE

Nothing else matters this week.

- [ ] **Call the Futsi owner.** Confirm Sunday Nov 15, two courts, 1:00–6:30pm.
- [ ] Confirm **how many courts** Futsi actually has — this sets the field at 12 or 8.
- [ ] Agree the money: comped, sponsor-swap, or flat rate.
- [ ] Get Futsi's **certificate of insurance** and a clear answer on whether you need your own rider.
- [ ] Send the written follow-up (in `pitch-futsi.md`) so there's a paper trail.
- [ ] Build the target list of 18–20 shops, ranked, with the right contact name for each.
- [ ] Grab the Instagram handle and put a placeholder event page on DripMap.
- [ ] Decide the start time. Recommendation is **2:00pm first whistle** — ask your two closest shop contacts to sanity-check it.

**Gate:** Do not pitch a single coffee shop until Futsi confirms.

### Roles — you need 7 people, not counting refs

| Role | # | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Tournament director | 1 | You. Keeps the schedule. Doesn't get sucked into any one job. |
| Check-in / merch | 2 | Wristbands, tees, extra tee sales, waiver checks |
| Scorekeepers | 2 | One per court. Scoresheet → spreadsheet → board. |
| MC / sound | 1 | Announcements, next-up calls, keeps the room warm |
| Photo / video | 1 | Every team, the final, the trophy lift |
| Referees | 3 | 2 on, 1 rotating off |

**The director role is the one that fails.** If you're at the check-in table at 2:15 you're already behind. Hand it off and walk the floor.
