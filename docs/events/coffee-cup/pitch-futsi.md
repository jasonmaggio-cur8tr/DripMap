# Pitch: Futsi (venue partner)

**Send this first. Nothing else starts until Futsi says yes to Nov 15.**

You're friends with the owner, so the written version below is a backup to the conversation, not a replacement for it. Have the conversation in person, at Futsi, on a slow afternoon. Bring coffee.

---

## The ask, in one breath

> "I want to fill your Sunday on November 15th with twelve Sacramento coffee & tea shops playing 3v3 street soccer for a trophy, and a couple hundred people watching. I run everything — registration, brackets, refs, promo, cleanup. You keep every dollar behind the bar. I need two courts from 1 to 6:30, and your name on the whole thing."

---

## What Futsi gets

**1. A dead Sunday afternoon turns into the busiest room in the building.**
60–80 players and 150–250 spectators, most of whom have never been inside Futsi. In an off-peak window that would otherwise earn nothing.

**2. All food and beverage revenue is theirs. 100%. No split, no ask.**
A packed room for five hours on a Sunday with a bar open is the immediate return, and I'm not touching it.

**3. A league funnel that's worth more than the day itself.**
Set up a sign-up table with a Coffee Cup discount code. These are exactly Futsi's customer: 25–40, urban, social, competitive, already sorted into ready-made teams with a rivalry. Convert three teams into a league season and that's real recurring revenue — far more than a Sunday's court rental. I'll push it from the mic and in the follow-up email to every player.

**4. Marketing reach Futsi can't buy.**
Twelve coffee & tea shops each post to their own audience for three weeks straight — team announcements, the bracket draw, game day, the trophy. Every one of those posts says Futsi. Coffee and tea shop audiences are the exact demographic for an indoor soccer facility, and they're being reached by the shops themselves, not by an ad.

**5. Name on everything, permanently.**
"The Coffee Cup at Futsi." On the back of every shirt, on the bracket board, on the banner, in every photo, on the DripMap event page. Year after year.

**6. Free content.**
Photos and video from the day, delivered to Futsi, free to use forever. Most facilities never get decent footage of a full house.

**7. Zero operational load.**
I run registration, seeding, brackets, referees, waivers, check-in, the schedule, the MC, the awards, promo and cleanup. Futsi provides the space, a point person for the day, and keeps the bar open.

## On the rate

Ask for it comped, and mean it — the F&B and the league conversions are the return, and a zero-cost first year is what makes a second and third year happen.

If he can't comp it, three options in order of preference:

1. **Comped year one, then a standing annual booking** at full rate once it's proven.
2. **He sponsors at the same number he charges.** Pay the $700, he puts $700 in as title sponsor, his logo goes where the title sponsor's logo goes. It's a wash on paper and he gets the top billing on the shirt.
3. **Flat discounted rate, paid up front.** Name a number you can carry — around $350 — and take it.

What you should not do is quietly accept full rate and then squeeze the shirts, the trophy or the photographer to pay for it. Those three are what make people come back.

---

## The bigger idea (raise this once he's warm)

The Coffee Cup is year one of two things.

**The Coffee League** — a six-week Wednesday-night coffee shop league at Futsi, running off the rivalries the tournament creates. Twelve teams, a season, a table, a champion. That's recurring league revenue for Futsi in a weeknight slot, and it's the reason to say yes to a one-off Sunday in November.

**And it travels.** Same format, same trophy, local venue partner — Portland, Seattle, Oakland, LA. Futsi is the flagship. Whatever we learn on Nov 15 is what the other cities run.
