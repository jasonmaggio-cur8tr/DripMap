## The scene (owner's brief, verbatim intent)

Liam Gallagher rides a Lambretta down wet Manchester streets to Cherki's shop — a lot of
focus on him riding in his parka on the classic scooter, looking very cool. He stops
outside the matcha bar and Cherki comes out.

> **CHERKI:** You get the shipment from Kyoto, Liam?
> **LIAM:** Yes twin, hand me the Peas.

They start the exchange. Cut to an all-black sporty e-motorcycle ripping down the same
streets Liam just came down. The camera is close on the rider's Nike TNs and pans up to
reveal him in all black, black hoodie, pulling a black shiesty over his face — Foden.
He speeds toward the hand-off. As Liam's hand extends with the bag of matcha, Foden zips
by, grabs it and is gone. Very fast e-motorcycle sound.

> **CHERKI:** Who just nicked my matcha!?

"Peas" is the callback to Cherki's Episode 1 hook. Keep the spelling — it is the joke.

## Constraint learned

Three riding stills failed the image content filter on the first phrasing. The trigger is
the combination of a masked rider and a theft. Rewording so the shot reads as motion and
costume rather than menace clears it. Worth remembering for the full episode: the robbery
beat sits close to a line the image model will not cross, so keep it playing as comedy.
