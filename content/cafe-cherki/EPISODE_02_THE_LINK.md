## Scenes

### Scene 1 — Under a railway arch, Ancoats canal, night (12 s)
The e-moto ticks as it cools. Rain drips off the brickwork. Foden peels the shiesty off,
breath fogging, the clear bag of Kyoto matcha on the seat. He rings Rashford. Split to
Nan's, Withington: Rashford flipping the sign to CLOSED, biscuit in his mouth.

> **FODEN:** Rash. I nicked the gear. **[proposed wording]**
> **FODEN:** What d'you want doing with it?
> **RASHFORD:** No matcha in Manchester. Sell it to the link that runs London.

### Scene 2 — Same arch / London, night (12 s)
Foden thumbs a contact saved as **TT**. Split to Thierry Henry under a striped awning,
camel coat, collar up, London drizzle, a black cab hissing past behind him.

> **FODEN:** TT. I'll be in London tonight with some food.
> **TT:** Meet me at Nan's in London. It's always quiet there.
> **TT:** Safe.
> **FODEN:** Safe.

### Scene 3 — London, night. Ride montage (12 s, no dialogue, bumper bed)
Orange rims flash across Tower Bridge. Big Ben in the mirror. A red bus swallows him and
spits him out at Piccadilly. He pulls up on a quiet corner: **NAN'S**, lace curtains, one
light on. Next door, **SAKA MATCHA**, green neon, and a queue of girls in matching claw
clips going down the street and round the corner. Foden looks at the queue. Looks at his
bag. Goes into Nan's.

### Scene 4 — Inside Nan's, London (22 s)
Doilies, a two-bar fire, a bell that doesn't ring. TT at a corner table with a pot of tea
and one cup. Foden sits, slides the bag across. TT doesn't touch it. He talks. Foden
doesn't.

> **TT:** Keep the single origin Kyoto bags coming. London is in a shortage.
> **TT:** Becks is opening posh cafés all over London. He's draining the supply.
> **TT:** Saka's got a queue round the block and he gives the pastries away for free. Nice boy. Too nice. **[proposed]**
> **TT:** And I hear there's a French kid in Manchester doing it properly. I would like to meet him. **[proposed]**

Foden's hand stops on the teacup. He says nothing. TT finally pulls the bag towards him.

### Scene 5 — Penthouse matcha café, top of a tower on the South Bank (10 s)
White stone, one long counter, floor-to-ceiling glass: Big Ben and the Thames below,
lights on the water. Becks in a leather jacket, curtains falling into his eyes. He
straightens his cup a quarter-turn. Sips. Phone.

> **BECKS:** Ship me all the matcha. Europe is about to be mine.

### Scene 6 — Café Cherki, Ancoats, after hours (12 s) **[proposed]**
Every light off but the counter. Cherki alone, whisking nothing, an empty tin beside him.
Phone buzzes: LIAM.

> **LIAM:** Twin. Kyoto's gone dry. Some cockney's bought the lot.
> **CHERKI:** *(long beat, sets the whisk down)* Then I go to London.
