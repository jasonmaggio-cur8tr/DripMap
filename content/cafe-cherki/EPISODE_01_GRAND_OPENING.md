# Café Cherki — Episode 1: "Grand Opening"

**Runtime:** 60 s · **Aspect:** 9:16, 1080×1920 · **Look:** plasticine claymation (see SERIES_BIBLE.md)
**Structure:** cold open → logo ident → story → cliffhanger card. DripMap overlay on every frame.
**Cast:** Rayan Cherki, Marcus Rashford, Phil Foden, Erling Haaland, Jack Grealish (phone), Harry Kane (phone), Wayne Rooney (phone), Doreen the cat.

Dialogue budget is ~130 words. Every line is short so the clay mouths can carry it.

---

## Cold open (0:00–0:03) — HOOK

**SHOT 1 · ECU · Cherki's hands**
A bamboo whisk moves through neon-green matcha in slow, absurdly precise figure-eights.
Rain hammers a window behind. Rings on every finger.

> **CHERKI (V.O., French accent, calm):** Manchester. Rains three hundred days a year.
> *(beat)* So I opened a matcha bar.

---

## Logo ident (0:03–0:05)

**IDENT · Café Cherki**
Hard cut. Chunky cream clay letters *CAFÉ CHERKI* on the blonde wood counter, matcha bowl
beneath. The whisk does one fast spin, steam puffs, the green neon flickers on.
**Sting:** kettle click → single deep bass hit. Two seconds. Same clip every episode.

**CAPTION (on cut back):** `ANCOATS · GRAND OPENING`

---

## Scene 1 — Café Cherki (0:05–0:13)

**SHOT 2 · WIDE · Café Cherki interior**
White-washed brick, blonde wood, three pastries in a glass case, one green neon sign:
*c'est de l'art*. Total silence except the whisk. Cherki stands behind the counter in an
oversized cream wool overcoat, boxy white tee, wide black trousers, chunky loafers,
tiny leather crossbody.

> **CHERKI:** No sugar. No music. No cow.
> *(gestures at the empty room)* Silence is… aesthetic.

**SHOT 3 · MED · Foden at the counter**
Foden, bucket hat, Stone Island badge, tracksuit, squints at a menu with two items on it.

> **FODEN:** You got a brew, mate?
> **CHERKI:** We have ceremonial grade.
> **FODEN:** …So no.

Foden picks up the price card. ECU: `£11`. His eyebrows leave the frame.

---

## Scene 2 — Nan's (0:13–0:28)

**SHOT 4 · EXT · Nan's tea room, Withington**
Terraced house front. Net curtains, hanging basket, a hand-painted sign: *NAN'S*.
Foden hurries in out of the rain.

**SHOT 5 · MED · Rashford pouring**
Floral wallpaper, doilies, gas fire glowing, framed photo of the Queen. Rashford in a
Barbour wax jacket over a cream cable knit, straight-leg jeans, Clarks Wallabees. He
pours from a pot in a knitted cosy and lays a custard cream on the saucer with total care.

> **RASHFORD:** Two sugars. No nonsense.
> **FODEN:** Rash. Cherki's opened a matcha shop.

Rashford freezes mid-pour. Tea keeps pouring. Overflows the cup. He does not look down.

**SHOT 6 · CU · Rashford**

> **RASHFORD:** A what?
> **FODEN:** Matcha. It's green. He whisks it. Eleven quid.
> **RASHFORD (slow):** Eleven pound. For a green brew. *In Manchester.*

He sets the pot down very gently.

> **RASHFORD:** He's takin' the piss.

A grey cat on the armchair meows.

> **RASHFORD:** Not you, Doreen.

---

## Scene 3 — Café Cherki, later (0:28–0:38)

**SHOT 7 · MED · Cherki plating**
Cherki places a single madeleine on a slate with tweezers. Adjusts it 2 mm. Nods.

**SHOT 8 · WIDE · Haaland enters**
Door opens. Haaland ducks under the frame: oversized grey hoodie, silky pyjama trousers,
slides with socks, hair tied back. He carries a bucket-sized thermos.

> **HAALAND:** Black coffee. Big.
> **CHERKI:** We don't do coffee.

Long stare. Rain. The neon buzzes.

**SHOT 9 · MED · Haaland leaves**
Haaland turns, takes the madeleine off the slate on his way out, says nothing.
Cherki looks at the empty slate.

> **CHERKI (whisper):** …C'est de l'art.

---

## Scene 4 — Nan's, the call (0:38–0:50)

**SHOT 10 · CU · Rashford picks up a chunky cordless house phone**
On the wall behind him a corkboard already has a Polaroid of Cherki's shopfront pinned
with a drawing pin. Foden is eating a second custard cream.

**SHOT 11 · SPLIT-SCREEN CALLS (three quick clay phone bubbles, 1.5 s each)**

> **GREALISH** *(in bed, alice band, eye mask pushed up)*: It's two in the afternoon, Rash.
> **RASHFORD:** I know.

> **KANE** *(gilet, measuring flour with a ruler)*: Is this about the oat milk again?

> **ROONEY** *(greasy spoon, spatula, glare)*: *(grunt)*

**SHOT 12 · MED · Rashford, jaw set**

> **RASHFORD:** Lads. There's a Frenchman selling green water for eleven quid.
> Round mine. Tonight. Bring a plan.

He hangs up. Foden, mouth full:

> **FODEN:** Bring a what?

---

## Scene 5 — Cliffhanger (0:50–0:57)

**SHOT 13 · WIDE · Café Cherki exterior, night**
Cherki flips the sign to *FERMÉ*, looks at his shop, exhales like an artist.
Rain. Neon reflects in the puddles.

**SHOT 14 · REVERSE · Across the street**
Through a rain-streaked car windscreen: three bucket-hat silhouettes. A custard cream is
passed between them.

> **RASHFORD (O.S.):** He wants aesthetic?
> *(beat)* …Let's give him aesthetic.

Wipers. Cut to black.

---

**CARD:** `NEXT TIME ON CHERKI'S CAPPUCCINOS` — sting (kettle whistle rising into a
single deep bass hit). Cream text on coffee-dark, tiny clay matcha whisk icon.

## Higgsfield production pipeline (Ep 1)

Locked models: **nano_banana_pro** for stills (character refs pinned via `image_references`),
**kling3_0** (pro, sound on) for image-to-video, 5 s clips from a `start_image`. Costs at
time of writing: 2 credits / still, ~12.5 credits / 5 s clip. Full episode ≈ 14 clips ≈ 175
credits plus stills.

### Locked style block (prepend to every prompt)

```
Handmade plasticine claymation stop-motion still, Aardman-style, visible fingerprints and
clay texture, slightly oversized heads, big expressive clay eyes, miniature handcrafted set
built from cardboard, felt and painted wood, warm tungsten practical lighting, tilt-shift
shallow depth of field, subtle 35mm film grain, stylised original caricature characters,
no text, no watermark, no logos, no football kits.
```

### Character lock (Cherki)

```
Young French man in his early twenties, light brown skin, short dark tight curls with faded
sides, slim build, sharp cheeky grin, small gold hoop earring, wearing an oversized cream
wool overcoat over a boxy white tee, wide-leg black tailored trousers, chunky black leather
loafers, tiny black leather crossbody bag, several silver rings.
```

### Character lock (Rashford)

```
Young Black British man in his mid twenties, deep brown skin, short hair with a sharp
line-up, faint goatee, strong jaw, warm tired eyes, athletic build, wearing an olive Barbour
waxed jacket over a cream cable-knit jumper, straight-leg indigo jeans, tan suede Clarks
Wallabee shoes, a flat cap hanging on the hook behind him.
```

### Assembly

1. Stills → approve look per shot → kling3_0 image-to-video 5 s (dialogue in prompt for lip-sync audio).
2. Trim each clip to the timing above in the video-editing workflow (higgsedit) and burn captions.
3. Insert the reusable ident clip at 0:03, then cut back into shot 2.
4. End card is a static still with the sting layered.
5. Top track: `overlay/dripmap-overlay-1080x1920.png` for the full duration, 100% opacity.
