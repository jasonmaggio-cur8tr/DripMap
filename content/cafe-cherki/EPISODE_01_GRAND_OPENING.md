# Café Cherki — Episode 1: "Grand Opening"

**Runtime:** 60 s · **Aspect:** 9:16, 1080×1920 · **Look:** plasticine claymation (see SERIES_BIBLE.md)
**Structure:** cold open → logo ident → story → cliffhanger card. DripMap overlay on every frame.
**Cast:** Rayan Cherki, Marcus Rashford, Harry Maguire, Erling Haaland, Jack Grealish (phone), Harry Kane (phone), Wayne Rooney (phone), Doreen the cat.

Dialogue budget is ~130 words. Every line is short so the clay mouths can carry it.

---

## Cold open (0:00–0:05) — HOOK

**SHOT 1 · Cherki in his chair, Dr. Evil**
A high-backed swivel chair, back to camera, in front of the rain-streaked window. It
turns. Cherki, cream flame-print knit, orange shades, one eyebrow up, slowly stroking a
bamboo whisk like a cat. He raises his pinky to the corner of his mouth.

> **CHERKI (thick French accent, villain-calm):** I am going to make Ps… selling
> matchas with cold foams, to all the Gen Z in Manchester.
> *("Ps" is pronounced "Pees". Spell it "Pees" in the TTS prompt.)*

Thunder. The neon flickers on.

---

## Logo ident (0:05–0:07)

**IDENT · Café Cherki**
Hard cut. Chunky cream clay letters *CAFÉ CHERKI* on the blonde wood counter, matcha bowl
beneath. The whisk does one fast spin, steam puffs, the green neon flickers on.
**Sting:** kettle click → single deep bass hit. Two seconds. Same clip every episode.

**CAPTION (on cut back):** `ANCOATS · GRAND OPENING`

---

## Scene 1 — Café Cherki (0:07–0:14)

**SHOT 2 · WIDE · Café Cherki interior**
White-washed brick, blonde wood, three pastries in a glass case, one green neon sign:
*c'est de l'art*. Total silence except the whisk. Cherki stands behind the counter in the
cream flame-print mock-neck knit, orange-tinted shades, wide black trousers, chunky
loafers, the tan tote parked on the counter like a pet.

> **CHERKI:** No couches. Card only. No cow's milk. No music.
> *(gestures at the empty room)* Silence is… aesthetic.

**SHOT 3 · MED · Maguire at the counter**
Maguire, enormous head under the low pendant light, navy tipped polo, squints at a menu
with two items on it.

> **MAGUIRE (broad Yorkshire):** Oi, can I get a cuppa, mate?
> **CHERKI:** We 'ave ceremonial grade, single source matcha.
> **MAGUIRE:** Bin man.

Maguire picks up the price card. ECU: `£11`. His eyebrows leave the frame.

---

## Scene 2 — Nan's (0:14–0:29)

**SHOT 4 · EXT · Nan's tea room, Withington**
Terraced house front. Net curtains, hanging basket, a hand-painted sign: *NAN'S*.
Maguire ducks in out of the rain, head first.

**SHOT 5 · MED · Rashford pouring**
Floral wallpaper, doilies, gas fire glowing, framed photo of the Queen. Rashford, hair
in neat cornrows, in a beige check hooded jacket over a white tee, straight-leg jeans,
Clarks Wallabees. A half-unpacked suitcase with a Barcelona luggage tag sits by the
armchair. He pours from a pot in a knitted cosy and lays a custard cream on the saucer
with total care.

> **RASHFORD (impossibly proper):** I'm back from Barcelona, and ready to sell some tea.
> **MAGUIRE (broad Yorkshire):** Rash, Cherki's opened a trendy matcha shop.

Rashford freezes mid-pour. Tea keeps pouring. Overflows the cup. He does not look down.

**SHOT 6 · CU · Rashford**

> **RASHFORD (slow, precise):** There's only one type of tea in Manchester.

He sets the pot down very gently.

> **RASHFORD:** He is, I'm afraid, taking the piss.

A grey cat on the armchair meows.

> **RASHFORD:** Not you, Doreen.

---

## Scene 3 — Café Cherki, later (0:28–0:38)

**SHOT 7 · MED · Cherki plating**
Cherki places a single madeleine on a slate with tweezers. Adjusts it 2 mm. Nods.

**SHOT 8 · WIDE · Haaland enters**
Door opens. Haaland ducks under the frame: leopard-print silk pyjama set,
slides with socks, hair tied back. He carries a bucket-sized thermos.

> **HAALAND:** Black coffee. Big.
> **CHERKI:** Matchas only, mate!

Long stare. Rain. The neon buzzes.

**SHOT 9 · MED · Haaland leaves**
Haaland turns, takes the madeleine off the slate on his way out, says nothing.
Cherki looks at the empty slate.

> **CHERKI (whisper):** …C'est de l'art.

---

## Scene 4 — Nan's, the call (0:38–0:50)

**SHOT 10 · CU · Rashford picks up a chunky cordless house phone**
On the wall behind him a corkboard already has a Polaroid of Cherki's shopfront pinned
with a drawing pin. Maguire is eating a second custard cream.

**SHOT 11 · SPLIT-SCREEN CALLS (three quick clay phone bubbles, 1.5 s each)**

> **GREALISH** *(in bed, alice band, eye mask pushed up)*: It's two in the afternoon, Rash.
> **RASHFORD:** I know.

> **KANE** *(gilet, measuring flour with a ruler)*: Is this about the oat milk again?

> **ROONEY** *(greasy spoon, spatula, glare)*: *(grunt)*

**SHOT 12 · MED · Rashford, jaw set**

> **RASHFORD:** Lads. There's a Frenchman selling green water for eleven quid.
> Round mine. Tonight. Bring a plan.

He hangs up. Maguire, mouth full:

> **MAGUIRE:** Bring a what?

---

## Scene 5 — Cliffhanger (0:50–0:57)

**SHOT 13 · WIDE · Café Cherki exterior, night**
Cherki flips the sign to *FERMÉ*, looks at his shop, exhales like an artist.
Rain. Neon reflects in the puddles.

**SHOT 14 · REVERSE · Across the street**
Through a rain-streaked car windscreen: three silhouettes, one head twice the size of
the others. A custard cream is passed between them.

> **RASHFORD (O.S.):** The mandem wants aesthetic?
> *(beat)* Say less.

Wipers. Cut to black.

---

**CARD:** `NEXT TIME ON CHERKI'S CAPPUCCINOS` — sting (kettle whistle rising into a
single deep bass hit). Cream text on coffee-dark, tiny clay matcha whisk icon.

## Higgsfield production pipeline (Ep 1)

Locked models: **nano_banana_pro** for stills (character refs pinned via `image_references`),
**kling3_0** (pro, sound on) for image-to-video, 5 s clips from a `start_image`. Costs at
time of writing: 2 credits / still, ~12.5 credits / 5 s clip. Full episode ≈ 14 clips ≈ 175
credits plus stills.

### Locked style block (prepend to every prompt)

```
Hyperreal claymation stop-motion still, puppets with realistic human proportions and
realistic facial anatomy sculpted in polymer clay, detailed sculpted skin with subtle
fingerprint texture, real glass eyes, individually sculpted hair strands, miniature
real-fabric costumes, miniature handcrafted set with real materials, warm tungsten
practical lighting, tilt-shift shallow depth of field, subtle 35mm film grain, in the
style of high-end hyperreal stop-motion feature films, no text, no watermark, no logos,
no football kits.
```

### Character lock (Cherki)

```
Young French man of North African descent in his early twenties, light olive-brown skin,
short dark tight curls on top with faded shaved sides, full short dark beard neatly trimmed,
roundish face, thick dark eyebrows, warm brown eyes, wide confident smile, orange-tinted
rectangular sunglasses, a cream mock-neck knitted jumper with a large abstract rust-orange
and brown flame-like print across the chest, black wide-leg trousers, chunky black leather
loafers, oversized tan leather tote bag, several silver rings.
```

Alternate look (Ep 2 onward): dark indigo raw-denim trucker jacket open over a white tee,
wide dark denim jeans with a tonal embroidered motif on one thigh, same beard and curls.

### Character lock (Rashford)

```
Young Black British man in his mid twenties, deep brown skin, hair in neat boxed cornrow
braids running front to back with a razor-sharp line-up and faded sides, thin moustache and
a small pointed goatee, strong straight brow, narrow serious eyes, small gold stud earring,
athletic build, wearing a beige tartan check hooded zip-up jacket with black and red
plaid stripes over a white tee, straight-leg indigo jeans, tan suede Clarks Wallabee shoes.
```

### Character lock (Maguire)

```
Tall broad white English man in his early thirties, pale skin, a comically large head with
a high prominent forehead, thick dark brown hair swept to one side, light stubble, small
close-set eyes, a huge toothy grin, thick neck, wearing a navy polo shirt with a tipped
collar, grey straight-leg jeans, black Adidas Samba trainers, holding a Yorkshire Tea mug.
```

### Assembly

1. Stills → approve look per shot → kling3_0 image-to-video 5 s (dialogue in prompt for lip-sync audio).
2. Trim each clip to the timing above in the video-editing workflow (higgsedit) and burn captions.
3. Insert the reusable ident clip at 0:05, then cut back into shot 2.
4. End card is a static still with the sting layered.
5. Top track: `overlay/dripmap-overlay-1080x1920.png` for the full duration, 100% opacity.
